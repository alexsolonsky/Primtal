## Product in one sentence

Primtal collects six private wellbeing answers, compares them with the participant's personal schedule, and asks permission before applying a specific calendar change.

The panel is the setup and control surface. Ambiguous is the participant's conversation and calendar interface. Background processing runs online after activation.

Use the [panel](https://primtal-agent.aicreatormax.chatgpt.site) with an authorised site account, or start in [Ambiguous](https://app.ambiguous.ai/chat) by sending `Hello` to Primtal in your own direct conversation.
