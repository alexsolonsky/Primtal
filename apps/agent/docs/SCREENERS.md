# Follow-up instruments

PHQ-9 and GAD-7 are implemented as optional follow-ups after all six daily questions. Each result is stored separately. No diagnostic algorithm is applied. A positive PHQ-9 item 9 pauses calendar suggestions regardless of total score; it does not establish imminent danger.

- PHQ-9 wording and scoring: [USPSTF form](https://www.uspreventiveservicestaskforce.org/home/getfilebytoken/sDvjG49sFm7t3f3CnZUuFw). Nine scored items, 0–3; total 0–27. Bands 0–4, 5–9, 10–14, 15–19, 20–27. Two-week recall. The unscored functional-impact question is not included in this demo.
- GAD-7 wording and scoring: [ADAA form, January 2026](https://adaa.org/sites/default/files/2026-01/GAD-7_Anxiety-updated_0.pdf). Seven scored items, 0–3; total 0–21. Bands 0–4, 5–9, 10–14, 15–21. Two-week recall.
- Reproduction terms: [Pfizer unrestricted public access announcement](https://www.pfizer.com/news/press-release/press-release-detail/pfizer_to_offer_free_public_access_to_mental_health_assessment_tools_to_improve_diagnosis_and_patient_care).

BAT-12, ASRS v1.1 6Q and a full technostress instrument are **not active**. Triggering these branches explains the missing questionnaire and asks the participant to explicitly skip or stop. No score is invented and the flow never silently treats them as completed. The daily focus question is not an ADHD assessment. Team clinical review and selection of the technostress instrument remain necessary for these extensions.
